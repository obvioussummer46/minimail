Three dots
minimal carries two tittles. minimail carries three. The mark is the word reduced to those three dots — and the one it gained takes the accent, which is the same dot the inbox draws against an unread thread.

minimail
minimal + i → minimail. The dots sit over letters 2, 4 and 7, so the gaps run short-then-long — the mark keeps that 2:3 rhythm rather than spacing evenly, which is what stops it reading as a loading indicator.
Tradeoff
Three dots is borrowed territory — overflow menus, typing indicators, ellipses. The uneven rhythm and the single accent dot are the only things making it ours, so neither is negotiable.
