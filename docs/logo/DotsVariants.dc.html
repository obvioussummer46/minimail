Mark studies
Six ways to set three dots
Same three tittles, six arrangements. The accent always falls on the third — the one the name gained.
1 · True rhythm
Gaps in the 2:3 ratio the tittles actually sit at. Off-balance on purpose.
2 · Even
Calmer, and one step from every overflow menu ever drawn.
3 · Baseline
The whole wordmark abbreviated: its tittles, and the line they sit over.
4 · Stair
Reads as arrival — mail landing down a list. Loses the wordmark link.
5 · Stacked
Same rhythm turned upright. This is the one the navigation borrows.
6 · Inverted
Solves the white-tile edge problem, but spends the accent on the field.
Where this lands
1 for the icon and 5 for the toolbar: the same three dots, rotated, so the button in the corner is visibly the app's own mark rather than a borrowed ellipsis. 3 is the one worth a second look if the icon needs more weight.
